# v2026.10.5 — Unicode lookaheads on the fast route, GCC -O2 builds that inline, a lighter Python match

**A trailing lookahead after a Unicode class takes the class route**: `\w+(?=,)` and `\p{L}+(?!é)` searched on
the general VM, at eight times the cost of the same shape over an ASCII class, and now run on the trailing-
lookaround walk. **GCC builds at `-O2` — the Python and Go bindings, and the Linux wheels — scan `.`, `.+` and
`[^,]+` without a call per byte.** **Python's `Match` is one allocation, and `match` / `search` / `fullmatch` and
the span accessors take their arguments without building a tuple.** `replace` reads its template once.

## Fixes

- **`replace` rejects a malformed template whether or not anything matches** (3703763): a bad reference (an
  out-of-range or unknown group, an unclosed `${`) over a subject without a match returned the subject
  unchanged; it now raises the error a matching subject raises, with the same message and offset, as Python's
  `re.sub` does.

## Performance

- **The trailing-lookaround route takes code-point class bodies** (67878c3): a search of `\w+(?=,)` on a short
  line 395 → 59 ns (Apple clang, arm64); instructions per search 6 373 → 837 on GCC 15 x86-64, 6 970 → 884 on
  GCC 14 arm64. ASCII-class bodies are unchanged.
- **The batched walk of `.`, `.+` and `[^,]+` inlines its UTF-8 width test under GCC `-O2`** (b1035df), where
  GCC had kept it out of line: instructions per count over 64 KiB, `[^,]+` −16 % / −12 % / −15 % (GCC 13 x86-64,
  GCC 15 x86-64, GCC 14 arm64), `.+` −33 % / −28 %, `.` −21 % / −16 %; the `-O3 -flto` duel `[^,]+` −5.6 %, `.`
  −8.5 %. Apple clang and clang 19 already inlined it. One other row moves: `\w+(?=,)` counted +2.3 % on GCC 15
  `-O2`.
- **`replace` reads its template once, before the walk** (3703763), as Python's `sub` and the C ABI's `real_sub`
  already did: −1.2 to −6.7 % instructions over 2 000 replacements with group references, on four compilers;
  a one-character literal template −8.4 % on GCC 15 and clang, +1.8 % on GCC 14 arm64.

## Bindings

- **Python.** A `Match` holds its spans in its own allocation (2bee5c6): `search` then `group` −12 %, `search`
  then `span` −18 %, `match` without groups −18 %, a `finditer` reading every span over non-ASCII text −30 %
  (per call, arm64). `match`, `search`, `fullmatch`, `group`, `start`, `end` and `span` take vectorcall frames
  (3812fdf): a further −7 to −13 % per call. Every rejected call raises the exception type `re` raises.

## std::regex compatibility layer

- **`match_results::format` and `regex_replace` share one replacement expander** (4f20369): the ECMAScript and
  sed rules were kept in three copies. `format` −1.3 to −1.7 % instructions per call on GCC; `regex_replace`
  +0.4 to +1.1 %.
