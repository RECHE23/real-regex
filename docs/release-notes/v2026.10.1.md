# v2026.10.1 — literals at the regex crate's pace, alternations near it, and a guard on what every call costs

**A literal on arm64 is found at the regex crate's speed, and an alternation within 12 % of it.** On the 500 KB
log corpus of issue #3, through the Rust binding against the crate, `hello` takes 16.0 µs against the crate's
16.5–18.5 (it was 29.3), and the ten-word alternation 75.5 against 67.5 (it was 88.8). **Iterating a pattern with
a group is up to three times faster**: `(info|error|warn)\s+\d{4}-\d{2}-\d{2}\s+req=[a-f0-9]+` 490 → 155 µs on
x86-64. **Two costs the September trains added to every call and every regex are gone**: a search wrote fifteen
members of state it rarely read, and every regex carried 1 069 bytes of alternation products in place. **A new
gate holds those costs**: `check-fixed-cost` pins the sizes of a regex and its search state, the instructions
of five short calls, and those of a walk that fills its groups.

## Performance

- **The literal pair filter rejects a 64-byte round with one test** (002e44a), as the byte scan already did.
  NEON has no movemask, so a mask per block cost a narrowing shift and a lane move, four a round. arm64:
  `hello` 29.4 → 16.1 µs, `maintainers` 29.5 → 16.6; x86-64 without AVX2 −31 % instructions.
- **The alternation fingerprint reads each block once and tests four blocks at once** (74d3dc7): its three
  lookups run on one load and the first two are aligned against the previous block's, as Teddy does. arm64:
  the ten-word alternation 87.5 → 81.1 µs, over the corpus's filler alone 49.2 → 40.2.
- **An alternation of four branches or more is batched** (4c4d39c): a walk keeps its scan between matches
  instead of re-entering the engine per match, unless the Aho-Corasick gate would take the subject, which the
  batch asks once and obeys. 81.1 → 73.9 µs (arm64), 44.0 → 39.6 M instructions (x86-64).
- **A confirmed inner-literal window fills its groups by the bounded backtracker** (5d01daa) rather than the VM,
  where the pattern is not one-pass: x86-64 find_iter 490 → 155 µs, replace 522 → 187, the first match
  1.23 → 0.38 µs; arm64 373 → 151.
- **An alternation's small set carries its eight lanes** (5cb034d), built once with the pattern: a short
  alternation search 862 → 793 instructions.
- **A search state builds its per-subject literal densities only when a literal search runs** (dfbb52d), and a
  **regex carries its Aho-Corasick automaton and probe pairs off the object** (4c4f01a): a short literal search
  609 → 591 instructions, `sizeof(real::regex)` 2 960 → 1 904 bytes, a SciLex lexer back to its 2026.9.9 memory.

## Gates and tooling

- **`check-fixed-cost`** (6055417, c8617f5, 034cde6): `sizeof` of a regex, its immutables and the search state,
  exactly, per ISA and standard library; callgrind's instructions for five short calls and one walk that fills
  its groups, within 2 %, keyed by system and GCC version, counted on CI's Ubuntu image natively or in a
  container (the probe's own instructions only, so the CPU's features do not move the count). Step 5e of
  full-local-gate and CI's preflight, which now runs on a pinned ubuntu-24.04 (4090d8c).
- **MISRA runs under the clang-tidy CI pins**, named once in SciForge's `lint/misra.mk`, which every ecosystem
  repository and CI's lint spine read (db020c1); SciForge pinned at v2026.10.0.
- The docs site's linkcheck tries a link three times before it counts as broken (b346122).
