# v2026.10.0 — the `std::regex` drop-in on REAL for most match flags, and four out-of-bounds or wrong answers fixed

**Four defects are fixed, two of them out-of-bounds reads.** The `std::regex` drop-in read past the first
element of a `std::deque` or `std::list` searched without results, and read the byte before a temporary copy
when `regex_replace` ran over an iterator range under `match_prev_avail`; under the ECMAScript grammar a
multiline `^` and `$` ignored a carriage return, so 10 417 of 31 251 lines of a CRLF log went unmatched where
libstdc++ and libc++ match them; and a destroyed regex left its lazy DFAs in the threads' caches until each
thread's next search. **The drop-in now answers on REAL where it used to build a `std::regex` on demand**:
`match_continuous`, `match_prev_avail`, `match_not_null`, `match_not_eol` and `match_not_eow` on a search or a
match, `format_sed` on a replace, and any non-contiguous range. It names the members `std` names
(`std::regex::icase`, `assign`, `match_results::format`, …), and the RE2 drop-in spells canned options,
anchors, error codes and `LazyRE2` as RE2 does. **Alternations of 64 literals or more are a trie in the lazy
DFA** (14 500 words followed by a digit: a warm search 7.7 s → 0.1 ms).

## Fixes

- **A non-contiguous range is searched on REAL over one contiguous copy** (2e228df). A search or match
  without `match_results` over a `std::deque`, a `std::list` or a reverse iterator read past the range's first
  element (an ASan heap overflow on a deque, wrong answers on a list; 9253ebb first routed it to `std`). The
  range is now copied once and searched on REAL, the positions handed back as the caller's iterators: the same
  answers as over a string, linear time, O(n) memory for the copy.
- **`regex_replace` over an iterator range under `match_prev_avail`** read the byte before its temporary copy
  (ASan, libc++ and libstdc++); it reads the caller's own range (e37c279). A search or match without results
  under the flag reads the caller's context too (ec19c94).
- **An ECMAScript multiline `^` and `$` end a line at a carriage return**, as libstdc++'s and libc++'s do
  (58d5ef5): a plain `real::regex` keeps its own anchors. Since 15ec2ef they are assertions of the engine and
  run on the lazy DFA again (a multiline `^\w+` over 1 MB of CRLF lines, arm64: 24.3 → 2.1 ms).
- **A destroyed regex frees its DFA sets** (a3d102c): a 1 000-word alternation left 9.07 MB behind after
  destruction, now 0. Another thread's cached set is still released at that thread's next lease or exit.
- A lookaround past its bound says the bound is 255 bytes (d5c2e95). A null C string stops a debug build and
  reads as the empty subject in release (5211568).

## The `std::regex` drop-in

- **Constraining match flags on REAL** for one search or match (15c9b74, bb2d55e, b637ff2):
  `match_continuous` is a match anchored at `first`; `match_prev_avail` a search from `first` with the
  character before it as context for `\b`, `\B`, a multiline `^` and a lookbehind (`match_not_bol` and
  `match_not_bow` are then ignored, as the standard says); `match_not_null` a search that accepts no empty
  match; `match_not_eol` and `match_not_eow` a rewrite of the pattern built once. Iterators and replace keep
  `std` for constraining flags, and `match_not_bol` / `match_not_bow` alone keep `std`. libc++ ignores
  `match_prev_avail` for `^` and never lets `\b` hold on an attempt at the end; REAL follows the standard and
  libstdc++ there. The exhaustive flag axis (99044b8) compares all of this with `std` on every push: 25 722 130
  cases against libstdc++, none serious.
- **`format_sed` on REAL** (67e4a0f): sed's rules, `$0` literal under sed. A final lone backslash is kept as
  libstdc++ and libc++ keep it and dropped as MS STL drops it, following the native `std` (d11e158).
- **The members `std` names** (8fbf9f0): `regex::icase` and the other class constants, every `assign` and
  `operator=` (strong guarantee, policy kept), the `initializer_list` constructor, `sub_match` ordering and
  comparisons with a character, `match_results::format` / `==` / `swap` / `max_size` / `get_allocator`, the
  `regex_constants::error_*` codes with `regex_error(code)`, and the token iterator's C-array field list.
  `imbue` and `getloc` are not supported, and no call reaches `std` in their place.

## The RE2 drop-in

- `RE2 re(p, RE2::Quiet)` and the other canned option sets, `RE2::ANCHOR_START` / `ANCHOR_BOTH` /
  `UNANCHORED`, `RE2::NoError`, and `LazyRE2` (0146283). `Options::set_log_errors(true)` writes RE2's message
  on a failed compile (06330ca). The page is now headed "RE2 compatibility layer" and lists what is not
  provided (`Match`, the named-group maps, `Extract` / `Rewrite`, the `*N` statics, the radix parsers).

## Performance

- **A literal alternation of 64 branches or more is a trie in the byte program** (7b55aab), and a lazy DFA
  may keep 65 536 states within its 64 MiB byte budget: `(?:14 500 words)[0-9]` over 32 KiB, warm search
  7.7 s → 0.1 ms and peak 78 → 27 MB (arm64).
- **A lazy DFA quits when its cache fills again before reading ten bytes a state** (a3099a7), and **marks
  the pcs its closure enters by generation** (3ebd7fa): the first search of a 14 500-word alternation 105 →
  50 ms.
- **The inner-literal code-point runs test an ASCII byte without the decoder** (79e6792): since c6b9556
  gcc kept the decoder out of line there, and `(\w+)_(\w+)` / `(\w+)@(\w+)` lost 32 % and 27 % on x86-64;
  both are now under their 2026.9.9 figures (x86-64 g++ 15.3.1 `-O3 -flto` 6.30 → 5.89 and 1.73 → 1.66
  ns/B; arm64 −19 % and −10 %).
