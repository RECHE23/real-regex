The SIMD candidate arc lands: alternation and quantified-class scans are mask-carried on both ISAs, and
both now out-run PCRE2-JIT for the first time — plus a dense-corpus regression from 7.22-7.25 fixed, and a
round of gate/harness robustness work that this train's own numbers exercised for real.

## Performance: the SIMD candidate arc

`run_alternation` and `run_fixed_shape` (a homogeneous `class{n}` — `[0-9a-f]{8}`, `\d{4}`) now scan a
16-byte block for candidates and verify them mask-carried, on both SSE2 (x86-64) and NEON (aarch64); the
scalar path (constexpr / other ISAs) is untouched. Alternation (`the|fox|dog`-shaped patterns) is **~1.45×**
faster on x86; the quantified-class line is **~2.7×** (x86, `[0-9a-f]{8}` 3.75 → 1.38 ns/B). Both now beat
PCRE2's JIT for the first time on their respective shapes — alternation **0.73× → 1.12×** PCRE2, quantified-
class **0.59× → 1.39×** — closing a gap the engine has trailed on since the very first PCRE2 comparison
(2026.7.17). Single-width lookarounds (`(?=[a-z])`, `(?<!x)`) are **18-28%** faster via an eval-time peephole
that skips the sub-VM scaffolding for a one-op body.

Numbers are two-leg (arm64 + x86-64), and the misses are said plainly, not rounded away: the arm64 leg of the
quantified-class line stayed flat across the arc's own internal iterations (no regression, but short of what
each step targeted for that ISA specifically); the x86 alternation line measured 2.02 ns/B against a 1.6
ns/B target for that item. Both are real wins over the pre-arc baseline — just not the ceiling each step
hoped for, and the arc's commit history says so at each point rather than only in the rollup here.

## Fix

- **The inner-literal route no longer steals a literal PREFIX.** A pattern whose match begins with a literal
  (`id=[0-9a-f]{8}`) belongs on the fast `find_prefix` path; since 2026.7.22 the inner-literal route's
  `inner_literal_prefix == 0` clause captured it too, running reverse-confirm machinery where `find_prefix`
  would do — **3.3× slower on a dense corpus** (246 → 100 µs on the archived repro, restoring the 2026.7.16
  level). The route now requires a genuine inner literal (offset ≥ 1); the date and email cases the route
  exists for are unchanged to the microsecond.

## Robustness

- The benchmark harness (fiche-1) is two-ISA-aware end to end, and documents the PCRE2 duel as
  ISA-dependent rather than a single cherry-picked number.
- `make version-check`'s benchmark-freshness warning did its job on this very release: `docs/BENCHMARKS.md`
  is stamped against 2026.7.25, and this train's own gains make those tables conservative — re-stamped at
  the next benchmark refresh, not re-measured wholesale just to bump a date.
- The SIMD arc's correctness nets pin the tail boundary (a subject shorter than one 16-byte block, a
  candidate inside the scalar tail) alongside the block-scan and skip-continuation paths.
- `include/real/engine/simd.hpp` — the ISA-exclusive mask primitives behind both scans — is excluded from
  the coverage floor with a documented reason (structurally uncoverable by a single-ISA CI runner, guarded
  instead by sanitize/fuzz/the twin ISA's own coverage); the floor itself did not move.
- The differential fuzz harness (`fuzz_compat`) now bounds a pattern's total counted-repeat load before
  compiling it — an unbounded `{n}`-storm was an ASan-allocator amplification, not a new divergence class,
  and was stalling the sanitized smoke for no additional bug found.
